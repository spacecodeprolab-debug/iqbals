Our history

Old Ahmedabad’s Mughlai table, open since 1947.

  1. 1947

    Open since 1947

    Iqbal Hotel opened in Jamalpur, Old Ahmedabad, in 1947, the year India became independent. It has traded under one name ever since: Iqbal Hotel, Since 1947.

  2. Old city

    A landmark of the old city

    Over the decades it has come to be widely recognised as one of Old Ahmedabad’s oldest and most iconic Mughlai eateries.

  3. The plate

    Mutton first

    The menu is built around mutton: handi, nihari, rogan josh, dal gosht, dabba gosht, arbi and paya, with biryani, butter chicken and a short seafood list alongside. Mutton and seafood dishes are served in a minimum of 400 gms.

  4. The glass

    Irani tea to finish

    A heavy Mughlai meal ends with a glass of Irani tea.

  5. Late nights

    A second door

    Iqbal Fastfood And Sweets keeps the family name open for late-night quick bites and sweets.

  6. Today

    Still serving from Jamalpur

    Same name, same banner: Proud to Serve.
